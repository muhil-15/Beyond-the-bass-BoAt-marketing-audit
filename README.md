# Beyond the Bass: A Digital Marketing & Competitive Positioning Audit of boAt Airdopes 131

An independent, public-source marketing audit examining product communication, digital discovery, competitive positioning, and potential opportunities for boAt Airdopes 131.

**Project type:** Digital Marketing Audit | Competitive Benchmarking | Consumer Positioning

**Research approach:** Public product pages, competitor pages, and selected Google search observations.

**Full case study:** [View on Notion](https://furry-grouse-9fb.notion.site/Beyond-the-Bass-BoAt-Airdopes-131-Marketing-Audit-4009089b7cb780cb9945c9c028483dd1)

---

## 1. Project Objective

Evaluate how boAt Airdopes 131 communicates its product value online, how it appears in selected search contexts, and how its positioning compares with selected competing earbuds.

This project translates publicly observable evidence into practical marketing recommendations. It does not claim access to boAt's internal advertising performance, customer data, conversion rates, or sales records.

## 2. Scope of Analysis

- **Product communication:** Review of features and specifications presented on the official product page.
- **Digital discovery:** Qualitative observations from selected Google search queries.
- **Competitive benchmarking:** Comparison with Noise Buds N1 and Boult Z40 using publicly stated product features.
- **Positioning:** Exploration of a potential student-focused value proposition as a hypothesis to validate.
- **Recommendations:** Suggestions for product-page clarity, communication, testing, and measurement.

## 3. Key Findings

### Finding 1: Product information consistency

The reviewed boAt product page displayed a Bluetooth-version discrepancy between different sections. This is a product-information clarity issue worth verifying against the current official specifications.

### Finding 2: Search discovery spans multiple channels

In the search snapshots reviewed, the official brand store and marketplace listings appeared in branded-search results, while broader category searches surfaced multiple brands and shopping or video results.

These observations do not prove nationwide rankings, market share, lost sales, or campaign effectiveness.

### Finding 3: Student-focused positioning is a hypothesis

Affordable earbuds may be relevant to students, but this project does not establish that students are an underserved segment or would prefer a student-specific campaign. Interviews, a short survey, and a controlled message test would help validate the hypothesis.

## 4. Competitive Benchmarking

The audit reviews publicly stated product features for:

- boAt Airdopes 131
- Noise Buds N1
- Boult Z40

Brand-published specifications are treated as claims made by the respective brands, not independently verified laboratory results. Prices, availability, ratings, and offers can change.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Clarify:** Verify and align product specifications across page sections.
2. **Compare:** Present relevant benefits in a clear, easy-to-scan format without unsupported superiority claims.
3. **Validate:** Test student-oriented messaging with target users before committing to a segment-specific campaign.
4. **Measure:** Define success metrics before testing, such as product-page engagement, add-to-cart rate, and purchase conversion rate, where authorized analytics access exists.
5. **Document:** Keep dated screenshots, source links, and a record of the method used for each observation.

These are recommendations for future testing, not claims that the actions have been implemented or will necessarily improve performance.

## 6. Methodology and Limitations

This is a qualitative audit based on publicly accessible information reviewed during the research process. Search results and product pages are dynamic and may differ by time, location, personalization, and device.

The project does not use internal campaign dashboards, advertising spend, click-through rates, return on ad spend, customer-level data, or verified sales data. No causal conclusions are drawn from search-result placement or brand-published specifications alone.
